A real-time smart platform connecting hotels, restaurants, and donors with verified NGOs to eliminate food waste and fight hunger.

Join as Donor / NGO Portal Login

1. Donors Post Surplus

Hotels, restaurants, and individuals list available fresh surplus food along with quantity, location, and expiry time.

2. Verified NGOs Claim

Admin-verified NGOs browse nearby available food postings and accept claims instantly for pick up.

3. Live Tracking

Track food pickup from Available $\rightarrow$ Accepted $\rightarrow$ Pickup Started $\rightarrow$ Delivered with automated expiry protection.
